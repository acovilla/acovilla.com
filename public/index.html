Andrés G. Covilla Martínez

PhD Research Fellow · Kristiania University of Applied Sciences, Oslo

I study how organizations adopt AI and what it takes for it to deliver real results rather than scattered experiments.

My doctoral research, in Communication and Leadership, examines how business leaders communicate and build authority in digital spaces, using computational methods such as deep-learning-based language analysis.

I came to research from business: I co-founded a marina and dock company in Colombia and worked in marketing, from a Start-Up Chile venture to an international education provider. Through Método con IA, I bring research-based insight on AI adoption to Spanish-speaking companies.
