Andrés G. Covilla Martínez

PhD Research Fellow · Kristiania University of Applied Sciences, Oslo

I'm interested in the dynamics of growing businesses and in how companies make a positive impact on society. That interest led me to start companies and, across several countries, to research and teach business, from marketing to analytics and communication. Creating, sharing knowledge and teaching is what I'm most passionate about.

I read a lot, because reading lets me live many lives and understand other points of view. I train, and I value the discipline it builds. I'm drawn equally to art and creativity, to the rigour of science and to what technology makes possible.

Right now I'm finishing my PhD in Oslo, on how business leaders build authority when they communicate online, and building Método con IA.
